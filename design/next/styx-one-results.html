Campaigns and reports, from brief to result

Follow-up to styx-one-place.html (frames 5, 6, 11). A campaign goes brief → draft you can edit → one approval for exactly what you see → live with a stop button → ended with its report. A report leads with the answer, says where every number came from, and is shared on a Styx address that keeps itself up to date.

1. Starting a campaign or a report: a short brief, or a starter

Choosing Campaign on the ask box turns it into a brief with the four things the AI always needs. Everything is optional: anything left out, the AI asks. Starters are one click to a filled brief.

  • Goal decides what Meta optimises for (sales, leads, visits, reach).
  • Budget is per day or in total, in the ad account’s currency; it becomes the cap you approve later.
  • Starters are written from what is connected: no Shopify, no “retarget shoppers”.

2. A campaign draft: see the ads as people will, change anything, approve exactly that

Each ad is drawn as it appears in the feed, with its picture from the store. Text and the plan can be changed right here: no message to the AI needed. What you approve is what you see, word for word.

  • Click any text to edit it; the dashed outline shows it is editable.
  • The plan’s numbers are fields: change the daily amount, days or countries, and the total updates.
  • Approve covers one campaign, these ads, this money, these countries, these dates. Anything else asks again.

3. Live: what it is spending, what it is earning, one button to stop

After approval the same card becomes the campaign’s live view. Numbers come from Meta every 15 minutes while Styx is open; each says how old it is. Pause everything stops every ad Styx made, at once, without asking the AI.

  • The meter is spend against the cap you approved, the one number that matters most.
  • Per-ad results show which ads to keep; “Do this” on a suggestion starts a change that asks before it spends.
  • Every action (launch, pause, budget change) is in the Record with who did it.

5. A report: the answer, how sure, where from, and what to do

Reports lead with the answer, then the numbers with last period beside them, a trend, the detail, and what to do next. Every number names its source and the time it was read.

  • Compared with the same length of time before, said as “▲ 18% on last week”.
  • The share card is the client link (frame 6): on a Styx address, not a raw server.
  • Keep it updating weekly re-runs the same report every Monday while Styx is open, and the link shows the newest.
  • Download a PDF for clients who want a file.

6. What the client opens: a branded link, nothing to install

The share server runs on a Styx address (share.heystyx.com, or the person’s own later). The page is the report, read-only, on any phone, with who shared it and when it was last updated.

  • No sign-in; noindex; can be turned off from Styx at any time.
  • The same address serves shared conversations and Styx’s sign-in pages for connected apps.
