Share a conversation like a doc.

No web app, no logins, no spaces. Share a lane or one message from Styx and you get a link anyone can read in a browser, like a Google Doc link. Team context is the business's own knowledge: brand voice, clients, pricing, how you work. Anyone on the team adds to it from Styx, everyone sees it there, and every agent can use it, in any project.

What stays
  • Share a lane, or one message, as a link: copy, change how long it lasts, turn it off
  • Teammates with Styx open a link in Styx and keep going from it
  • Team context: add to it from any message or write it yourself; everyone on the team reads it in Styx, and every agent in every project can use it
What goes
  • The signed-in web app: spaces, members and roles, the inbox
  • Rooms, waking agents, plans and boards
  • Asks, tasks, owners, lane status sharing, agent proposals

3. Team context

One place in Styx for what the business knows. Add to it from any message (Add to team context) or write an entry; edit it, mark it out of date. It belongs to the team, not to a project, so the brand voice or a client's rules reach every agent in every repo. An agent pulls what it needs by asking ("what's Acme's brand voice?"); a person reads it right here. Any entry can also be shared as a link.
