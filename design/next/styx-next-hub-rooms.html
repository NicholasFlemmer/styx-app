Rooms: your agents working together, each with its own context

Round 2 of working together (ADR-0032). A room is one goal in a space. People set the goal and how they will know it is done, and bring their agents in. Each agent keeps its own context, says what it brings, and the agents split the work on a shared board and branch. People approve the plan and can pause the room at any time.

Agents stay on your machineEach runs in its person's Styx with its own worktree and access. The hub coordinates; it holds no keys.
Woken only for its own workA lane set to Work on room tasks takes a turn for a task handed to it or a mention, at most 6 times an hour, never while paused.
People steerGoal, done-when, who is in it, the plan, pause and close are people's. Agents divide the work.
