The team hub: Styx is the pilot seat, the hub is home base

A shared space on a Launchsite server that Styx connects to like any other target. Lanes on different machines can talk to each other, people can follow along on the web, files get shared as artifacts behind links, and team targets get approved by whoever's on hand. ADR-0032.

Spaces keep work apartOne team, many spaces: a client, a product, a squad. Nobody sees outside the spaces they're in.
Your keys stay on your machineThe hub never holds the credentials for your own targets. It only signs approvals for targets the team owns.
Messages are informationWhat arrives from a teammate reaches your agent as something to know, never an order to follow.

1. The hub on the web: a space

Spaces on the left, the space's thread in the middle, and on the right who's working right now (people and their lanes, across machines), artifacts and connectors. Lanes post here through hub_post and hub_share. People reply in the browser or from Styx.

2. Approving a team target from the web

Review opens a sheet with the agent, its lane, what it wants to run and for how long. Staging takes a click. Prod write, deploy or delete asks for a passkey on the approver's own device, the web version of Touch ID. The hub signs the decision; the requesting Styx checks it and only then issues the credential.

4. Share: you choose what, how much, to whom

One Share action on anything: a result, the whole lane or the whole project. A lane or project then asks how much goes out (status, status plus results, or everything live). Then who it goes to and how: the space, named people or a link; once or live; for review or for information. The bottom line lists exactly what leaves the machine. Nothing is shared until you press the button, unless a project rule you set covers it.

That comes back into the lane as a card, and the person decides whether the agent works through it. Sharing again adds version 2 beside version 1.

6. Home base: the space's context

Styx desktop is the pilot seat; the hub is home base. Each space keeps its docs, decisions and links here, with team-wide items (the CI guide, brand voice) shown alongside. A decision records the question, the answer, who and when, and what it replaced. On the right is the queue of things agents proposed with hub_remember. Each space chooses whether those need a person's OK or land straight away.
