An IDE, organised around the work.

IDEs are built around files: open one, edit it, run it. When agents do the typing, the thing you manage is a piece of work, which Styx already calls a lane: one task, its agent, its branch, the conversation, the changes, a preview, the checks and the access it was given. So the frame of Styx becomes the work, and the editor, terminal, files and diff become instruments on it. Nothing you have today goes away. Every screen below keeps its features; what changes is where they live and how they read.
