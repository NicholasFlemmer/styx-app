Promote: a second mode, for getting what you built to people.

Building is half of shipping. The other half is getting it in front of people and finding out whether they came. Today that means a second set of tools, logins and dashboards, with nothing tying an ad back to the code it sells. Promote is a mode of Styx, beside Build, the way Claude has Code, Design and Chat. It stands on its own: a brand, its accounts and its campaigns, with or without a codebase. Link a brand to a Build project and the two can talk; leave it unlinked and they never do. Everything below is the campaign Styx ran for itself on 3 October 2026, drawn as it would look inside Styx.

A mode beside BuildThe switch in the title bar changes everything below it. The agents, Access, the audit log and Usage are shared; nothing else is.
Brands, then campaignsThe rail lists brands (Styx, Good Shout) where Build lists projects. A brand holds its website, its accounts and its campaigns, by whose move it is.
Instruments for promotionCreative, Tracking, Launch and Results are the tabs on a campaign, the way Preview, Changes, Code and Terminal are on a lane.
Spend is the new riskCreating things paused is an ordinary grant. Going live, raising a budget or publishing a tag needs Touch ID, and the grant carries the cap. The broker refuses anything above it.
Checks are code, not judgementSafe zones, tracking that respects consent, EU advertiser fields, merge-never-overwrite: Styx runs them, the agent does not improvise them.
Talking is opt-inA brand links to Build projects if you want. Linked, Promote can ask Build for a fix and hear when it lands, and Build can offer “announce this release”. Each direction has its own switch.

Tracking: ready to measure, or not

Before a campaign can optimise for anything, the account, the identity and the pixel have to exist and the site has to report the right thing. The top half is the setup, with the steps only you can do marked as your move and opened in the right Meta screen. The bottom half is Styx’s own check: it loads the live site as a first-time visitor, blocks every request to Meta so nothing is counted, and shows what would have been sent.

TodayThis was an hour in a separate Chrome window: a portfolio search, a Page wizard, a passkey, a Tag Manager import whose default would have deleted 16 tags, and a consent bug found only by testing. The double-counted download was the same check, failing.

Launch: one page, one decision, a cap on the money

The campaign comes back on paper like any finished work: what will run, where, for whom and for how long. Styx has already created all of it paused on Meta and read it back, so this page is what Meta holds, not what the agent meant to send. Going live opens the spend sheet. It is the same access sheet as a production write, with the budget written into the grant: the agent can never spend past it, and the broker pauses the campaign at the cap.

TodayThe campaign was built by a script, created paused, read back field by field in chat, then switched on with three API calls. The EU advertiser field failed the first attempt; Styx knows it is required for EU countries and asks for it here.

The other side of the link
