The **Changes** tab is a task's work as one page you can sign off: where it stands, what it was for, and every change the agent made, grouped by the message that asked for it. From there you land it, ask for more, or take back the part you don't want.

## What a turn is

A turn is one message from you and everything the agent does in reply, until it stops and waits for you again. Styx records the state of the task's files at the start and at the end of every turn. That record, a checkpoint, is what lets you see exactly what one turn changed and put it back.

Checkpoints are kept as hidden git refs in your repository (under `refs/styx/checkpoints/`). They are never on your branch, never pushed, and don't touch your index or your commits. They're cleaned up when the task is archived or its worktree is removed.

## In the chat

When a turn changes files, it ends with a result card in the chat: the agent's last reply, how long the turn took ("Done in 4 min"), how many files changed with lines added and removed, and pictures of your app before and after if it was running in [Preview](/docs/using/preview). The card has two buttons:

- **Show changes** opens that turn's diff.
- **Undo this turn** puts the files back (see below).

There's no accept step. If you carry on, the turn is kept; its receipt says "Kept" with the time.

The page opens with where the task stands (**Ready to land**, **Still working**, **Waiting on you**…), the task itself, which agent did it and in how many turns, and whether another lane has changed the same files ("Also changed in" and the branch, or "No other lane touches these files"). Then comes what the agent last said.

Under **What it did, turn by turn**, each turn is titled by what you asked, with its file and line counts. **Show** fetches its diff; **Undo this turn** takes it back.

Edits that no turn accounts for, such as changes you made in your own editor, are listed separately under **Changes not tied to a turn**.

At the bottom is the decision:

- **Land** brings the work into `main`. See [Land, publish and deploy](/docs/using/land-publish-deploy).
- **Ask for changes** puts the cursor in the chat's message box, so you can tell the agent what to do next.
- **Review hunk by hunk** opens the hunk review (see below).

## Undo a turn

**Undo this turn** asks first: "Put the files back as they were before this turn?" **Undo it** goes ahead; **Keep it** cancels.

What it does:

- **It restores the task's files to how they were before that turn.** Files the turn changed or deleted are written back; files it created are deleted. Files your `.gitignore` excludes are left alone.
- **It takes everything after that point with it.** Undoing an earlier turn also undoes every later turn, and any edits you made in the worktree since. Those turns are marked undone.
- **It doesn't touch git history.** It makes no commit and removes none. If the agent committed during the turn, the commit stays, and the undo shows up as uncommitted changes on top of it.
- **It waits for the agent.** You can't undo while a turn is running or waiting on you ("Wait for the agent to finish before undoing").

The agent isn't told that you undid a turn. If it matters for what comes next, say so in your next message.

<Callout type="warn" title="Undo rewinds the whole worktree">
  Undo puts the whole worktree back to a moment in time. To drop one change and keep the rest, revert the hunk
  instead.
</Callout>

## Revert a single hunk

To keep most of a turn and drop one part, use the hunk review.

<Steps>

1. **Turn on edit tracking.** Open **Settings › Editor** and turn on **Track agent edits (diff review)**. It's off by default because watching every edit can slow Styx down; until it's on, the review says "Agent edit tracking is off".
2. **Open the review.** On the Changes tab, press **Review hunk by hunk**. In the **Code** tab, the bar over the editor ("3 hunks from Claude") has **Review** too.
3. **Revert what you don't want.** Move through the hunks with <Keys k="j" /> and <Keys k="k" />, press <Keys k="r" /> to revert the one in focus, and <Keys k="Mod+Enter" /> when you're done.

</Steps>

The agent has already applied its edits, so there's nothing to accept: a hunk is either kept as it is or reverted. **Revert all** reverts every hunk from that agent, and **Mark reviewed** marks what's left as seen.

## Ask for changes

Most of the time the quickest fix is to tell the agent. **Ask for changes** puts you in the chat. You can also point at the problem: [Select to fix](/docs/using/preview#select-to-fix) in Preview sends the element and a picture, and selecting on a [design](/docs/using/design) screen does the same for a design.
